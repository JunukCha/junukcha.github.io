Junuk Cha

Computer Vision & Deep Learning Researcher · Currently on the job market

junukcha@gmail.com

I am a computer vision researcher, and I am currently looking for new research positions. Most recently, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the AMI Lab, KAIST (Aug. 2025 – Aug. 2026), working with Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh. I received my combined M.S./Ph.D. from the Artificial Intelligence Graduate School (AIGS) at UNIST in the UVL Lab, advised by Prof. Seungryul Baek, and my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UNIST in 2021.

During my Ph.D., I interned at Adobe (San Jose) and Inria (Nice). After graduating, I worked as a postdoc at KETI before joining KAIST.

Research interests

  • Computer vision and deep learning
  • Human-centric AI — reconstruction, generation and recognition of humans, hands and their interactions
  • AI for robotics — egocentric humanoid control, robot pose estimation
